BiuBiu Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

BiuBiu Pop is a fun sound-effect and shooting simulator for entertainment purposes only.

1. We collect no data

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from your device. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs. Game progress, achievements, theme and preference settings are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded.

2. Permissions

This app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, or the microphone.

When you turn on the flashlight feature, iOS requires camera permission in order to control the device torch (a system limitation). This permission is used solely to switch the flashlight on and off. The app never takes, saves, or uploads any photo or video, and never accesses your photo library.

3. Subscriptions and payment

In-app subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store. Payment and billing information is handled by Apple; we cannot access your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

4. Network and third parties

Apart from retrieving subscription product information from Apple, all features run locally on your device. We do not share data with any third party.

5. Children's privacy

This app is for entertainment only, contains no inappropriate content, and collects no user information, including that of children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the content and the effective date on this page.
