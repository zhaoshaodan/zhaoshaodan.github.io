❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I identify a mushroom?

Open the App → tap the camera or photo-library button → take or choose a clear photo of the mushroom → wait a few seconds for the result. For best accuracy:

  • Photograph the whole mushroom, including the cap, gills, stem and base;
  • Use good, natural lighting and keep the subject in focus;
  • If possible, add a second photo showing the mushroom's underside.
Can I eat a mushroom the App says is "Edible"?
No — never rely on the App alone. Results are AI-generated and may be wrong. Mistaking a toxic species for an edible one can be fatal. Never eat any wild mushroom or plant based solely on the App's output. The App is a learning and reference tool; always confirm with a qualified local expert before consuming anything.
Why did I get an "Unknown" result?

The AI could not identify the subject with acceptable confidence. This can happen with blurry photos, unusual angles, rare species, or non-mushroom subjects. Try a clearer, well-lit photo showing the full specimen.

How many free identifications do I get?

Every device gets 5 free identifications per month. The counter resets automatically at the start of each month. Unlimited identification is available with a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, then open Settings → Restore Purchases in the App. If it still doesn't work, email us and we'll help.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open the App's Settings → Manage Subscription, or on your iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I get a refund?

All purchases are billed by Apple. Request refunds through Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Photos are sent for identification, processed in memory, and never stored on our servers. A small thumbnail of each identification is saved only on your device in your history, and you can delete it anytime by swiping in the History tab. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why does identification need an internet connection?

The AI analysis runs on a secure server rather than on your device — this keeps the App small and results accurate. Identification requires an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular).

🛠 Troubleshooting

  • App won't open or crashes: make sure iOS is up to date (the App requires iOS 17+), then reinstall the App. Your history is stored locally and reinstalling will clear it.
  • "Network error" when identifying: check your internet connection and try again. If the problem persists for more than a few minutes, email us.
  • Camera button does nothing: check that the App has camera permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  • Photo library is empty: allow full access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → FungiLens.

❓ 常见问题

如何识别蘑菇？

打开 App → 点击相机或相册按钮 → 拍摄或选择一张清晰的蘑菇照片 → 等待几秒查看结果。为获得最佳准确度：

  • 拍摄整朵蘑菇，包括菌盖、菌褶、菌柄和菌托；
  • 使用良好的自然光，并保持主体对焦；
  • 如有可能，补充一张展示蘑菇底部的照片。
App 显示"可食用"，我可以吃吗？
不可以——绝不能只依赖 App。 结果由 AI 生成，可能出错。把有毒物种误认为可食物种可能致命。切勿仅凭 App 结果食用任何野生蘑菇或植物。本 App 是学习与参考工具；食用任何东西前，务必咨询合格的当地专家。
为什么结果是"未知"？

AI 无法以足够的置信度识别拍摄对象。这可能由照片模糊、角度特殊、物种稀有或拍摄对象并非蘑菇导致。请尝试使用光线充足、展示完整主体的更清晰照片。

每月有多少次免费识别？

每台设备每月可获得 5 次免费识别。计数器会在每月月初自动重置。订阅后可解锁无限次识别。

如何在新设备上恢复购买？

请确保登录的是原购买时使用的同一个 Apple ID，然后在 App 中打开设置 → 恢复购买。如果仍无法恢复，请发邮件给我们，我们会协助你。

如何管理或取消订阅？

打开 App 的设置 → 管理订阅，或在 iPhone 上前往设置 → [你的姓名] → 订阅。可随时取消；访问权限持续到当前计费周期结束。

如何申请退款？

所有购买均由 Apple 计费。请通过 Apple 申请退款：访问 reportaproblem.apple.com 并使用你的 Apple ID 登录。

你们会保留我的照片吗？

不会。照片仅用于识别，在内存中处理，绝不会存储在我们的服务器上。每次识别的一张小缩略图仅保存在你的设备上的历史记录中，你可以随时在"历史记录"标签页中滑动删除。详情请参阅我们的隐私政策。

为什么识别需要联网？

AI 分析在安全服务器上运行，而不是在你的设备上——这样可以让 App 保持轻巧并确保结果准确。识别需要有效的互联网连接（Wi-Fi 或蜂窝网络）。

🛠 故障排查

  • App 无法打开或崩溃：确保 iOS 为最新版本（App 需要 iOS 17 及以上），然后重新安装 App。你的历史记录存储在本地，重新安装会清除它。
  • 识别时出现"网络错误"：检查网络连接后重试。如果问题持续超过几分钟，请发邮件给我们。
  • 相机按钮无反应：检查 App 是否拥有相机权限，路径为设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 相机。
  • 相册为空：请在设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → FungiLens 中允许完整访问权限。
