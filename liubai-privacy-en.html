Liubai Privacy Policy

Liubai is a self-discipline tool that helps you put down your phone and stay present. All data is stored locally on your device.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

This app runs fully offline, requires no account or sign-in, contains no ads, and does not integrate any third-party analytics or tracking services. Your focus records, daily challenges, achievements, and personal settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

The "Deep Focus" feature uses the iOS "Screen Time" capability. Only after you grant authorization, and only during a focus session, it temporarily shields the apps you select. This authorization takes effect only while you use Deep Focus, and we do not read or collect your screen usage data.

This app offers an optional "Liubai Pro" subscription. Payments are processed by Apple through your Apple ID, and we do not collect or store any payment information. Your subscription status is securely managed by Apple.

By design, when you uninstall this app, all local data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

You can use the app with peace of mind and enjoy a private, focused, and simple experience.
